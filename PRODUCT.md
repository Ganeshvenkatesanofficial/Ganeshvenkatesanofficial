# Product Specification: Ganesh Venkatesan Profile

## Register

Engineering Identity & AI Builder Showcase

## Audience

Engineering leaders, AI healthcare founders, open-source collaborators, recruiters, and contact center architects visiting Ganesh Venkatesan's GitHub profile. They need to understand his core capabilities—**A2A (Agent-to-Agent) architectures, ADK (Agent Development Kit) frameworks, Contact Center automation, Healthcare clinical workflows, and local desktop copilots (DeskMate)**—with experience across **Thunai AI**, **Sarvam AI**, and **Zoho**.

## Product Purpose

Present an evidence-first, high-impact engineering profile. A visitor immediately grasps:
1. Ganesh's focus on building real-world AI systems: verifiable **A2A Contact Center & Healthcare AI** and **DeskMate** (an offline AI desktop assistant).
2. Deep proficiency with LangGraph, Model Context Protocol (MCP), tool-calling multi-agent loops, and clinical RAG pipelines.
3. Industry experience and contributions across [Thunai AI](https://www.thunai.ai/), [Sarvam AI](https://www.sarvam.ai/), and [Zoho](https://www.zohocorp.com/).

## Brand Personality

- **Technical, Ambitious, and Grounded**: Focused on real architectures that ship and run locally or in production, rather than hype.
- **Evidence-First**: Grounded in deterministic replay, EHR/tool verification, and auditable compliance.
- **Collaborative**: Open to open-source contributions and pairing with engineering teams.

## Anti-References

- Generic, lifeless badge walls without real architectural context.
- Inflated claims not backed by inspectable repositories or code.
- Broken links, misaligned badges, or untyped placeholders.

## Core Pillars

- **A2A Contact Center & Healthcare AI**: Agent-to-Agent protocols for automated triage, clinical intake, and verifiable patient workflows.
- **DeskMate**: Flagship privacy-first offline AI desktop assistant built with Flutter, FastAPI, Ollama, and SmolAgents.
- **Agent Workflows & GitOps**: Multi-agent orchestration, tool calling, and automated deployments with LangGraph and ArgoCD.
